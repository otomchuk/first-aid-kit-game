What’s in the kit

Every item in the game is real equipment we purchase for Ukrainian defenders. Your donation turns this game into a real first aid kit for the frontline.

  • CAT tourniquet

    Controls severe bleeding from the limbs.

  • KrovoSpas hemostatic gauze

    Packs deep, bleeding wounds.

  • Trauma shears

    Provide quick access to wounds through clothing.

  • Compression bandage

    A pressure dressing to control bleeding.

  • Occlusive chest seal

    Seals penetrating chest wounds.

  • Emergency blanket

    Helps protect an injured person from hypothermia.

  • Nasopharyngeal airway

    Helps maintain an open airway.

  • Splint

    Immobilizes fractures and dislocations.

  • Dressing pack

    For wound and burn dressings and antisepsis.

  • Gloves

    Protect both the responder and the injured person.

  • Decompression needle

    For decompression of tension pneumothorax.
